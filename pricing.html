Straight answers

The questions procurement always asks.

Are call volumes hard caps?
No. They are guides. A busy month is not a penalty — we tell you the overage rate up front, in writing, before you sign.
What is the contract length?
Monthly rolling after the first three months. The kill switch works instantly and without a ticket, so you are never trapped with something misbehaving in front of clients.
What happens to our data if we leave?
Exported in a usable format and deleted from our systems within 30 days. Self-hosted clients keep everything, because it never left.
Do you train models on our data?
No. Zero retention is in the DPA, not just on the website.
Why are you cheaper than a person?
We are not a replacement for your receptionist. We are what answers at 19:41 on a Friday when there is nobody there, which is when a good proportion of enquiries arrive.
Are these prices final?
They are a starting position. We will revisit after the first few clients when real call volumes and margins are known — existing clients keep the price they signed at.

Founding client terms.

The first firm in each sector gets setup waived and two months at cost, in exchange for a named case study once it works.
