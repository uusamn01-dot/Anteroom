Oels · Financial services

It books the adviser. It never advises.

The advice boundary is the whole design. Anteroom captures the enquiry, answers factual questions about your firm, and books time with somebody qualified — and it says it cannot advise, out loud, at the start of every conversation.

A mortgage broker and a wealth manager screen for completely different things.

Different questions, different systems, different escalation signals. The configuration is built around your firm and handed to you to edit — it is not a finance-flavoured version of a generic script.

Illustrative. Your real question tree is built from your intake, and you can edit it yourself afterwards.

Guidance, not advice, and the difference is engineered.

The regulatory risk in this sector is not that the AI is rude. It is that it says something that constitutes a personal recommendation.

Disclosure first

“I am an automated assistant. I cannot give advice, but I can book you in with an adviser” — hard-coded, every call.

Recommendation requests refuse

Asked whether they should do something, it declines and books instead. It will not speculate about affordability, suitability or returns.

Blocklist you control

Specific products, providers or phrases your compliance officer wants nowhere near an automated conversation. Checked before the model sees the turn.

We help you evidence good outcomes.

In plain English rather than jargon: the Duty asks you to show that customers understood what they were getting and that vulnerable customers were treated properly. Both of those are things a written record answers.

Consumer understanding
Every call opens with a disclosure the customer hears, and the log records that it happened. You can show the disclosure rate across every call in a quarter.
Vulnerable customers
Signals of financial distress, bereavement, ill health or confusion trigger a handover to a person. The trigger, the time and the destination are all logged.
Consumer support
Nobody sits in a queue at 19:41 to be told the office is shut. The enquiry is captured and booked, out of hours, on the first ring.
Named accountability
During onboarding you name who at the firm signs off intake changes. Nothing goes live without that person approving it, and the approval is recorded against their name.

The advice gap is a regulator-endorsed problem.

The FCA estimates millions of UK adults are underserved, including a large group with meaningful investable assets who are not getting advice. Firms need to handle far more inbound enquiries without adding advisers, while staying firmly on the guidance side of the line.

That is precisely the shape of this product: more enquiries triaged and booked, none of them advised. Book thirty minutes and we will build it against a real enquiry from your firm.
