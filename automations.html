The work your team does by copying between two windows.

Client onboarding, document chasing, conflict checks, matter opening, reporting. We sit with whoever does the work, watch for the moment they copy something from one window into another, and automate that.

We do not ask what you want automated.

People describe the work they find annoying, not the work that is expensive. So we sit with whoever actually does it and ask them to show us their week.

1 — Watch a real week

A day in your office, following the actual process rather than the one in the manual. We are looking for the copy-paste moments.

2 — Map it back to you

You get the map before anything is built, with the time cost of each step written on it. You decide what is worth automating.

3 — Build and hand over

Built in n8n, which self-hosts and has a visual editor your team can actually open. You are not locked out of your own workflow.

Because Zapier cannot run inside your network.

This is the single most important tooling decision in the stack. Any automation built on a tool that cannot self-host quietly breaks the promise that your data stays with you.

Self-hostable
Runs on a small VPS or your own server, in Docker. Genuinely a solved problem — this is the easy end of self-hosting.
Visually inspectable
Your team can open the workflow and see what it does. No black box, no support ticket to find out why something fired.
Custom code where needed
n8n handles roughly the first ninety percent. For anything needing real logic, a database or an unusual API, we write a small Node or Python service.
Yours at the end
The workflows are documented and handed over with a runbook. If you stop working with us, they keep running.

Quoted per build, not per seat.

Automation work varies by an order of magnitude depending on how many systems are involved, so we scope it rather than inventing a tier for it.

Typical range: a single well-defined workflow across two systems is a small build. A full onboarding chain touching case management, accounts, e-signature and email is a larger one. We give you a fixed price after the mapping session, and the mapping session is chargeable only if you do not proceed.
