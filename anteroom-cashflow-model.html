Reading this honestly

The quota column is the whole model. Everything else is arithmetic. One new client a month means roughly one signed deal from perhaps ten serious conversations, which is thirty or forty firms contacted. Professional firms take six to twelve weeks to decide. Work backwards from that before raising the sliders.

Founding clients suppress early cash on purpose. They pay no setup and only cover costs for two months. That is the price of a named case study, and the model shows exactly what it costs you.

Project work is the lever that changes everything. Set it above zero and watch break-even move. One £4,000 automation build is worth ten months of a Practice subscription. The recurring revenue is what makes the business valuable; project work is what keeps it alive while the recurring base grows.

What is not in here: your own time, VAT, corporation tax, bad debt, or the month a client's integration breaks and you lose a fortnight fixing it. Treat the cash line as optimistic by a comfortable margin.
