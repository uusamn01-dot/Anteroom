Outside the perimeter

These are not equally hard, and pretending otherwise burns clients.

Overclaiming here is the one mistake that ends a relationship permanently, so here is the real difficulty of each layer.

Easy

Automations

n8n in Docker on your server. A solved problem. No caveats.

Moderate

Retrieval and chat

Qdrant plus a local open-weight model via Ollama or vLLM. Good enough for search and summarising your own documents. Needs a GPU for reasonable speed.

Hard

Voice

Local speech-to-text is fine. Local speech that does not sound obviously synthetic, inside an 800ms budget, is a genuine engineering problem. We will not promise a fully air-gapped voice receptionist until we have built one and heard it.

The middle option most firms accept: automations, retrieval and all document storage entirely on your infrastructure, with only the voice layer using a processor under a signed DPA with UK or EEA residency and zero retention. We say exactly that rather than implying everything is air-gapped.
