Data

Where it lives and how long we keep it.

Residency
All processing and storage in a UK region, or the EEA where a component requires it. Named in the DPA, not left to a support ticket.
Retention
Call transcripts and structured intake data for the period you set during onboarding. The default is 90 days. Audit log entries are kept longer because they are the compliance artifact.
Model training
Your data is never used to train any model, ours or a third party's. This is contractual.
Deletion
On request, or automatically at the end of the retention period. Confirmed in writing.
Export
Transcripts, structured data and the audit log, in a usable format, at any time and without charge.
