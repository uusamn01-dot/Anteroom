What this notice covers

This notice covers personal data we collect about visitors to this website and people who contact us or book a demonstration.

It does not cover personal data we process on behalf of a client firm when their Anteroom system handles a call. In that situation the client firm is the controller and we are the processor, and the terms are set out in the data processing agreement rather than here.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no cross-site tracking. Your theme preference is held in memory for the session only and is forgotten when you close the tab. If we add analytics, we will use a cookieless product and update this notice before doing so.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing. We use a small number of suppliers to run the business — email hosting, calendar scheduling and website hosting — and they process data only on our instructions. Our current sub-processors for client-side processing are listed on the trust and security page.

How long we keep it

  • Demo enquiries that do not become clients: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client correspondence: for the length of the relationship and 6 years afterwards, to meet limitation periods and tax obligations.
  • Anything you ask us to delete: deleted on request, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Where it is stored

In the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. Where a supplier operates outside that area, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data erased, where we have no continuing reason to hold it;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • ask us to restrict processing while a dispute is resolved;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

Email enquiries@oels.dev and we will respond within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, by post at Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF, or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. Material changes affecting people we already hold data about will be notified by email.
