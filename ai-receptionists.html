Knowing when to stop talking.

Every supplier engineers what the AI says. Almost nobody engineers what happens when it should not speak at all. In regulated work that is the part that matters.

Disclosure is hard-coded

The opening turn is a fixed message, not a prompt instruction. The model cannot skip it because it never gets the chance to.

Blocklist runs first

Forbidden topics are checked before the model sees the turn. Your firm edits the list; it is enforced absolutely.

Escalation is a classifier

Distress and safeguarding detection runs alongside the conversation, not inside it. A model asked to notice distress will sometimes not notice.

Every escalation writes a log entry with the trigger, the timestamp and where the call went. That record is the artifact your compliance officer actually wants, and it is exportable.
