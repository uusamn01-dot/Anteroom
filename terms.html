Terms of service

What we provide, what we explicitly do not, and how either of us ends it.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between Oels (“we”) and the firm that engages us (“you”). Where you sign an order form or proposal, that document and these terms together form the agreement. If they conflict, the order form wins.

2. What we provide

We provide software and services for handling enquiries and automating administrative processes, which may include:

  • an automated reception system (Anteroom) answering voice calls and web chat;
  • workflow automations between your existing systems;
  • retrieval tools over your own documents;
  • deployment of any of the above on infrastructure you control.

3. What we explicitly do not provide

We do not provide legal advice, regulated financial advice, insurance mediation, or medical or clinical assessment of any kind.

Our systems perform intake and triage only. They capture what a caller says and route it to a qualified person at your firm. You remain solely responsible for any advice given to your clients, and for your own regulatory obligations to the SRA, the FCA or any other regulator.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Naming a person at your firm who approves intake configuration before it goes live.
  • Reviewing the scripts, question trees and escalation rules we configure, and telling us if anything is wrong for your practice.
  • Making sure you have the right to give us access to the systems and documents you connect.
  • Keeping your own account credentials secure.
  • Meeting your own regulatory and professional obligations. We help you evidence them; we do not discharge them.

5. Data protection

Where we process personal data on your behalf, we do so as your processor under the data processing agreement, which is signed before any system goes live. Our handling of your data is described on the trust and security page.

6. Fees and payment

  • Subscription fees are billed monthly or annually in advance, as set out in your order form.
  • Setup and project fees are billed as agreed in the relevant proposal.
  • Call volumes stated in a plan are guides, not caps. Where usage materially exceeds the guide, the overage rate in your order form applies — and we will have told you that rate before you signed.
  • Invoices are payable within 14 days.

7. Ending the agreement

  • After an initial three month period, either side may end the subscription with 30 days' written notice.
  • You may suspend all automated call handling instantly at any time using the kill switch, without notice and without contacting us.
  • Either side may end the agreement immediately if the other commits a material breach and does not fix it within 14 days of being told.
  • On termination we export your data in a usable format and delete it from our systems within 30 days. Self-hosted deployments retain everything, because it never left your infrastructure.

8. Service availability

We aim for high availability but do not guarantee uninterrupted service. Where a component depends on a third party — a telephony carrier, a model provider, your own case management system — an outage there may affect the service. Specific availability commitments, where agreed, are in your order form.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

Subject to that, our total liability in any twelve month period is limited to the fees you paid us in that period, and neither side is liable for loss of profit, loss of business or indirect loss. [LIABILITY CAP TO BE CONFIRMED ON REVIEW]

10. Confidentiality

Each side keeps the other's confidential information confidential and uses it only to perform the agreement. For us this includes everything we see inside your firm during process mapping, which is often more sensitive than the data itself.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
