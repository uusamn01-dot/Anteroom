Data processing agreement

The summary. The full agreement is signed before any system handles a real call.

Draft for review. This document was prepared as a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by a qualified solicitor. It must be reviewed, and the bracketed details completed, before Oels collects personal data from anyone. Do not rely on it as legal advice.

Last updated: 22 August 2026  ·  Version 0.1 (draft summary)

This page summarises the DPA in plain English so your compliance officer can assess it quickly. The executable agreement is a separate document, provided on request and signed before go live.

Roles

When our systems handle enquiries for your firm, you are the data controller and we are the data processor. You decide what is collected and why; we process it only on your documented instructions.

Subject matter and duration

ItemDetail
Subject matterHandling inbound enquiries, capturing structured intake data, and automating administrative processes
DurationThe term of the services agreement, plus the agreed deletion window
Categories of data subjectYour prospective and existing clients, and your own staff
Categories of personal dataName, contact details, the substance of the enquiry, call audio and transcripts
Special category dataNot intentionally collected. Callers may volunteer it — see below.
On special category data. A caller describing a family matter or a health problem may disclose special category data without being asked. Our systems do not solicit it, escalation halts intake when it appears in a safeguarding context, and it is covered by the same residency, retention and deletion terms. If your use case involves routinely processing special category data, tell us before go live — it changes the assessment.

Our obligations

  • Process personal data only on your documented instructions.
  • Ensure everyone authorised to process it is under a duty of confidence.
  • Apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
  • Not engage a sub-processor without telling you and giving you the chance to object.
  • Help you respond to data subject rights requests.
  • Help you with impact assessments and with breach notification.
  • Delete or return the data at the end of the agreement, as you choose.
  • Make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance, and allow audits.

Residency

All processing takes place in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area. Where a sub-processor operates outside that area, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement, and the mechanism is named in the agreement rather than left vague.

Retention and deletion

  • Transcripts and structured intake data: the period you set at onboarding. Default 90 days.
  • Audit log entries: retained longer, because they are the compliance artifact. Period agreed with you.
  • On termination: exported in a usable format, then deleted within 30 days with written confirmation.

Model training

Your data is never used to train any model, ours or a third party's. Zero retention is contracted with model providers, not merely configured.

Sub-processors

The list depends on your deployment and is set out in an annex to the agreement. Self-hosting removes most of it. The current typical list is published on the trust and security page, and you are told in advance of any change.

Security measures

  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Access on a least-privilege basis, reviewed when anyone joins or leaves.
  • Multi-factor authentication on all administrative access.
  • Audit logging of configuration changes, with the approving person recorded.
  • Documented backup and restore, tested rather than assumed.

Breach notification

We notify you without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with the information you need for your own 72-hour obligation to the ICO.

Requesting the full agreement

Email enquiries@oels.dev and we will send the executable version. You do not need to be a client to read it, and we would rather you read it early than late.
