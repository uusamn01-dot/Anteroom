Oels  /  Law firms

For law firms

Intake and triage. Never advice, and it says so first.

Built around your practice areas rather than a generic legal script. It discloses that it is automated at the start of every call, and the moment a caller describes something that needs a person, it stops asking questions and transfers.

Configured per practice area

Conveyancing, family and personal injury are not the same conversation.

Different qualifying questions, different routing, different escalation triggers. A single generic legal-intake script is how suppliers make one product serve everyone — and how firms end up with intake that fits nobody.

Illustrative. Your real question tree is built from your intake, and you can edit it yourself afterwards.

The bright line

It does not give legal advice. Ever.

Not as a prompt instruction that a model might drift past, but as engineering it cannot route around.

Disclosure first

“I am an automated assistant, not a solicitor” is the hard-coded opening turn on every single call. The model never gets the chance to skip it.

Advice questions deflect

Asked for a legal opinion, it says that is a question for a solicitor and offers to book one. It does not hedge, guess, or have a go.

Everything is logged

Disclosure, questions asked, answers captured, and where the call went. Exportable and timestamped.

Safeguarding

The escalation promise.

Distress, safeguarding concerns and urgent matters reach a human immediately. This is the most important thing on this page and the reason most family firms take the call.

Detection runs alongside
A classifier watches the conversation in parallel. It is not the model deciding for itself whether something is serious, because a model asked to notice distress will sometimes not notice.
Intake halts
No further qualifying questions are asked. Somebody describing domestic abuse is not asked for their postcode.
Transfer, not a callback
Straight through to your duty solicitor, on the same call, while the caller stays on the line.
You set the threshold
The confidence level at which it escalates is a slider in your control panel, not a setting you email us about.
Regulatory notes

Two things worth raising before you ask.

Personal injury. The SRA places extra restrictions on unsolicited approaches and on paid referral arrangements in PI work. Anteroom handles inbound enquiries only — it never makes outbound approaches, and we do not sell or broker leads. If your PI intake involves a referral arrangement, bring your compliance officer to the scoping call.

Confidentiality. Enquiry details are client-confidential from the first word. UK or EEA residency, a signed DPA before go live, and a retention period you set. Self-hosting removes the question entirely. Full detail on the trust page →
